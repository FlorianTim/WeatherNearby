# User manual

This manual is generated from `docs/public-site/` and rendered twice: into
the app (offline, under `assets/help/`) and onto the public website. Replace
the content when you derive an app from the template — not the generated
files.

## Getting around

- **Greet me** — the example screen. Replace it with your first real feature.
- **Gear icon** — the settings hub: language, appearance, help, privacy and
  data, Pro status and support.
- **Info icon** — about and help: the website, the FAQ, the changelog, the
  open-source licences and the support mailboxes.

{{screenshot:02-settings}}

## Language and appearance

Both settings offer **Follow the device** as their first option, and that is
also the default. An explicit choice overrides it and is remembered.

Changing the language takes effect immediately — including in this manual,
which is bundled once per language.

## Your data

Everything the app stores lives in a database on this device. There is no
account and no server.

Two separate actions can take it back, and they do different things:

- **Delete app data** — clears all content, keeps your settings.
- **Reset settings** — puts language, appearance and every other setting back
  to its default, keeps your content.

Both are on the **Privacy and data** page and both ask before they act.

## Getting help

- The FAQ in this app answers the most common questions offline.
- **About and help** has the support mailbox, a feedback address, and a bug
  report that attaches the app version and device details — never your
  content.
- If the app failed to start, the recovery screen offers the same report.
